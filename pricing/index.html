AI Build

Creates and changes your 3D design

What it does
Turns a sentence or a photo into an editable 3D booth: size, booth type, walls, flooring, colors and furniture. It also writes art direction for you.
What you get
A 3D design in the studio that you keep editing, branding and rendering.
What it uses
1 AI Build action per request.
Included
50 (week) · 150 (month) · 450 (year). Extra: 10 for $5.

AI Render

Photographs your finished design

What it does
Turns your current 3D view into an ultra-realistic photo of the booth on a real show floor, then can animate that photo into a 10-second video.
What you get
An image or video file for decks, pitches and social. It’s a picture, not an editable 3D design.
What it uses
1 image credit per image, 1 video credit per video.
Included
4 / 8 / 30 images and 2 / 4 / 10 videos. Extra: $5 per image, $10 per video.
