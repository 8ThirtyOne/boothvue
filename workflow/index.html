Step 1 · Brand it

Make it unmistakably yours.

Type your web address and AI reads your site: your name, tagline, colors, logo and visual style go straight onto the walls, counters and signs. The booth looks like your company, not a template.

  1. Open the Brand tab, enter your website and click Get Brand.
  2. Pick pictures from your site for the walls, or upload your own logo and artwork.
  3. Fine-tune the name, tagline and colors if you want to.
  4. Pick materials and lighting to match the mood of your show.

Step 2 · Describe it

Start with a sentence, or a photo.

Tell AI what you have in mind. It picks the size, booth type, walls, flooring and furniture, and builds it in 3D in your brand colors in seconds.

  1. Click AI Build at the top of the studio.
  2. Describe the booth in a sentence, or add a photo of one you like.
  3. Click Build my booth. The design is ready to edit.

Prefer to start from scratch? Pick a size and booth type instead.

Lounge sofa
03
Step 3 · Shape it in 3D

Click, drag and adjust anything.

Fine-tune the layout like moving furniture in a room. Every piece has real-world sizes, and you can walk the aisle at eye level to see it the way visitors will.

  1. Choose the booth type (inline, corner, peninsula or island) and a size.
  2. Click any item to select it, then drag it across the floor. Double-click to rotate.
  3. Add counters, screens, seating, plants and signs from the 53-item library.
04
Step 4 · Render it

Frame the shot and save a 4K render.

Orbit around the booth, step into the aisle at eye level, and save a crisp render from any angle. These were rendered straight out of the app without retouching. Select one to open that design and make it your own.

Step 5 · Picture it

Your booth, imagined on the show floor.

AI Render turns your design into a photo-realistic concept image or 10-second video clip, imagined on a show floor. It’s an AI visualization of where your booth could go, not a build plan, so your exhibit house will confirm what can actually be built. Drop it into investor decks, board presentations and pitches, win sign-off on your concept, and promote your booth before the doors open.
