The workflow

From blank floor to finished booth, visualized in one place.

Other tools stop at a floor plan or start from a finished render. BoothVue covers every step in between, so you go from an idea to a photo-realistic visual without switching apps or waiting on a 3D artist.

STEP 1

Describe it

Type what you want or add reference photos. AI picks the size, walls, floor, colors and furniture.

AI Build · photo to 3D
STEP 2

Shape it in 3D

Click anything to move, resize or swap it. Choose the booth type and construction system.

53-item library · real sizes
STEP 3

Brand it

Drop in your logo, artwork, fonts and colors. Light it the way the show floor will.

Graphics · lighting · materials
STEP 4

Render it

Walk the aisle at eye level among visitors and save a 4K render from any angle.

4K renders · plan and side views
STEP 5

Picture it

Generate an AI concept image or video clip of how your booth could look, right in the app, using your render as the reference.

Step 5 · Picture it

Your booth, imagined on the show floor.

AI Render turns your design into a photo-realistic concept image or 10-second video clip, imagined on a show floor. It’s an AI visualization of where your booth could go, not a build plan, so your exhibit house will confirm what can actually be built. Drop it into investor decks, board presentations and pitches, win sign-off on your concept, and promote your booth before the doors open.
