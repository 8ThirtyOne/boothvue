FAQ

Good to know.

How much does AI Render change my design?

You choose, with three levels. Stay true to my design keeps your booth exactly and makes it look like a real photo. Enhance my design keeps the same booth and layout but adds premium finishes, lighting and graphics. WOW me! lets AI reimagine it as an award-winning flagship, far beyond your 3D model. Your logo, typeface and brand colors stay exact at every level.

What’s the difference between AI Build and AI Render?

AI Build designs; AI Render photographs. AI Build gives you an editable 3D booth in the studio. You can move, resize, brand and change everything it makes. AI Render takes the view you framed and creates a finished, ultra-realistic picture or video of it for presentations. That picture isn’t editable 3D, so make your changes with AI Build or by hand first, then render. With Concept Upgrade on, AI Render also enhances the design well beyond your 3D model, like a professional exhibit designer would.

Do I need design or 3D experience?

No. You can start from a sentence or a photo, and change anything by clicking it. Every step is guided, from the first layout to the final photo-realistic visual.

Can I use the AI images, videos or specs to build my booth?

No. BoothVue is for concept visualization only. AI-rendered images and videos are artistic interpretations, not construction documents, and must not be used to build from. BoothVue does not provide official schematics, CAD or fabrication drawings, or engineering specs. The Specs panel gives general sizes and dimensions from your 3D model, as a starting point for the conversation. Your exhibit house or booth builder produces the final drawings and confirms what can be built.

Is this a CAD or fabrication tool?

No. BoothVue is for concept visualization: showing what your trade show presence could look like, for investors, presentations, pitches and internal sign-off. It doesn't produce CAD drawings, engineering or manufacturing details. When you're ready to build, share your visuals with an exhibit house as a starting point. You can also give them a 4K render, a GLB 3D model and a summary of approximate sizes, and they will produce the construction drawings.
