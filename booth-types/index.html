02

Corner

2 open sidesBack wall + 1 side partition
Typical sizes (ft)
  • 10 × 10
  • 10 × 20
  • 20 × 20
Best for
Catching traffic from two aisles at the end of a row.
Designer tip
Angle the counter toward the corner so it greets both aisles at once.
Design a corner booth
03

Peninsula

3 open sidesBack wall only
Typical sizes (ft)
  • 20 × 20
  • 20 × 30
  • 20 × 40
Best for
Bigger brands that want presence without a full island.
Designer tip
Use the back wall as a hero graphic and keep the front open for walk-in demos.
Design a peninsula booth
04

Island

4 open sidesCenter wall + hanging sign
Typical sizes (ft)
  • 20 × 20
  • 30 × 30
  • 40 × 40
Best for
Flagship presence, visible from every aisle on the floor.
Designer tip
A hanging sign is your beacon: it is how people find you from across the hall.
