Do I need to install anything?

No. BoothVue runs in your web browser on a laptop or desktop. A large desktop or laptop screen is highly recommended. On a phone or small tablet, the 3D view and controls are too small to design comfortably.

How do the AI features work?

AI is built in and included with every pass, with nothing extra to sign up for. There are two kinds. AI Build creates and changes your editable 3D booth from a sentence or a photo, and uses one AI Build action per request: 50 with a week pass, 150 with a month, 450 with a year. AI Render turns your finished 3D view into a photo-realistic image or a 10-second video, and uses image and video credits. Need more AI Build? Add 10 actions for $5 under Buy extras.

How much does AI Render change my design?

You choose, with three levels. Stay true to my design keeps your booth exactly and makes it look like a real photo. Enhance my design keeps the same booth and layout but adds premium finishes, lighting and graphics. WOW me! lets AI reimagine it as an award-winning flagship, far beyond your 3D model. Your logo, typeface and brand colors stay exact at every level.

What’s the difference between AI Build and AI Render?

AI Build designs; AI Render photographs. AI Build gives you an editable 3D booth in the studio. You can move, resize, brand and change everything it makes. AI Render takes the view you framed and creates a finished, ultra-realistic picture or video of it for presentations. That picture isn’t editable 3D, so make your changes with AI Build or by hand first, then render. With Concept Upgrade on, AI Render also enhances the design well beyond your 3D model, like a professional exhibit designer would.

How do I get the ultra-realistic image or video?

Once your booth is designed, click Generate. AI uses your render and your art direction to create a photo-realistic image or a 10-second video clip of your booth concept, right in the app. Each pass includes credits: 4 images and 2 videos for a week, 8 images and 4 videos for a month, or 30 images and 10 videos for a year. Need more? Buy extra images for $5 each or videos for $10 each under Buy extras. They’re added to your pass and can be used until it ends.

Can it pick up my brand automatically?

Yes. Type your website address in the Brand tab and click Get Brand. AI reads your public home page and fills in your company name, tagline, brand colors, logo and a short description of your visual style, then offers pictures from your site to put on the walls. AI Build and AI Render follow that style. You can change anything afterward. It uses 1 AI Build action.

Do I need design or 3D experience?

No. You can start from a sentence or a photo, and change anything by clicking it. Every step is guided, from the first layout to the final photo-realistic visual.

Is this a CAD or fabrication tool?

No. BoothVue is for concept visualization: showing what your trade show presence could look like, for investors, presentations, pitches and internal sign-off. It doesn't produce CAD drawings, engineering or manufacturing details. When you're ready to build, share your visuals with an exhibit house as a starting point. You can also give them a 4K render, a GLB 3D model and a summary of approximate sizes, and they will produce the construction drawings.

Do I need an account?

Yes, once you buy a pass. Sign in with the email you used at checkout and we'll send you a one-time sign-in link, so there's no password to remember. Your account holds your pass, your remaining image and video credits, and your generated images and videos.

Where are my designs saved?

When you're signed in, your designs save to your account automatically, so you can open them on any computer. You can also send a view-only share link to a colleague or investor.
